    About me（简介）

    Qing Xia received his Ph.D. degree in June 2025 from the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Xi’an Jiaotong University (XJTU), where he was supervised by Prof. Dr. Yibao Li. Prior to that, he obtained his B.S. degree from the same school at XJTU in 2019. Recently, he was selected for the TopMinds program and joined Huawei.

    Research（研究）

    My research interests mainly lie in Shape topology optimization in 3D printing and multi-scale multi-physics coupled computation. My research interests also include computer vision on the digital twin, entropy analysis and numerical analysis. Since 2019, I have published 43 SCI papers, 32 of them in JCR Q1 journals, 7 chosen as Highly Cited paper by Web of Science.
